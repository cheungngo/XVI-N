\begin{equation}
S_{D,\lambda}=S_D+\lambda I_t,
\qquad
\tfrac12(S_{D,\lambda},S_{D,\lambda})
=B_D+\lambda(S_D,I_t)+O(\lambda^2),
\end{equation}
where $B_D=\tfrac12(S_D,S_D)=-\frac\kappa2\int c^\nu B_\nu+\cdots$ (convention sheet).
If $Q_U I_t=0$ with $Q_U$ from $S_U$, then
$(S_D,I_t)=(V_\sigma,I_t)+O(\kappa^2)$.
For antifield-independent $I_t$ and $V_\sigma$, the displayed antibracket gives
$(V_\sigma,I_t)=0$ identically (both antifield derivatives vanish). Any nonzero
contribution in the $\lambda$ identity must come from antifield-dependent pieces of
the completion, not from the antifield-independent seed alone.
Present revision: completion $Q_U I_t=0$ Unresolved, so the $\lambda$ identity is
recorded structurally only.
