\paragraph{Scaling audit (arbitrary prescribed mass).}
\label{sec:scaling}
The frozen contract fixes the preparation data $f,g,\sigeps$ and the observation
region $U$ for each instance (XII-D/XI-B plateau conditions; Gate~N-Ins.1). It does
\emph{not} declare a free length-scale parameter $L$ that may be varied after the
instance is chosen. Rescaling to $X=(x-x_*)/L$ would change $\operatorname{supp}f$,
$\operatorname{supp}g$, and the relative geometry of the $g$-dip and the $\sigma=1$
plateau; whether such a change stays inside a single frozen instance is not
granted by the contract.

\textbf{Verdict.} The localized scaling route of Track~B3 is \textbf{unavailable}
as an automatic extension of the present instance. A future proof that shrinking
the geometry is permitted, and that a normalized constant
$|W(m,L)/W(0,L)-1|\le C(mL)^2$ stays controlled, would reopen it. Until then,
arbitrary prescribed $m>0$ remains outside the small-mass interval of
Section~\ref{sec:smallmass}.
